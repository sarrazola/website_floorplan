How to review AI virtual staging for an empty room

Prepare a room photo and check furniture, openings and scale before sharing an AI staging concept.

Quick answer

Virtual staging adds a visual furniture concept to a room photo. Use AI Home Planner & Room Design to explore an arrangement, then compare it with the real space and label it clearly as a visualization.

Step by step

  1. Photograph the room clearly

    Show the floor, major walls, doors and windows. Avoid extreme angles that hide circulation paths or distort the room.

  2. Choose a practical purpose

    Decide whether the room is a bedroom, office or living area. Start with the main furniture rather than a large decorative wish list.

  3. Check the physical constraints

    Look for covered doors, moved windows, impossible furniture sizes and missing architectural features. Measure the actual space before selecting real furniture.

  4. Share the source with the concept

    Keep the unedited photo and identify the result as virtual staging. Use the image to discuss options, not to imply the furnishings are included.

A practical example

For an empty living room, compare the sofa position with the entry door and window. Reject a version that blocks circulation even if its styling looks attractive.

Before you use the result

Generated furniture and proportions may be inaccurate. A staging image is not a survey, installation plan or proof that a specific product fits.
