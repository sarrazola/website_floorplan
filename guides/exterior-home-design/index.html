Visualize an exterior redesign without losing the real structure

Use an exterior photo to explore colours and materials while checking windows, rooflines and access.

Quick answer

An AI exterior concept can help compare visual directions. Start with a clear photo and verify that the generated result preserves the building’s important features before discussing any real work.

Step by step

  1. Capture the whole facade

    Include roof edges, openings and the ground connection. A straight, well-lit view makes changes easier to compare.

  2. Choose one design direction

    Explore a restrained colour or material change first. Keep a record of the original so you can separate styling changes from structural changes.

  3. Inspect the building details

    Check window count, door placement, roof shape, drainage and access paths. Reject a concept that invents or hides a structural element.

  4. Discuss feasibility separately

    Share the source and concept with the relevant professional before purchasing materials or changing the building. Verify actual colours with physical samples.

A practical example

Compare two facade colour directions using the same source photo. Check that neither output changes the number or size of windows.

Before you use the result

The image does not establish structural feasibility, permits, material availability or a construction budget. Local requirements and site conditions need separate review.
