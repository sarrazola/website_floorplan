How to turn a 2D floor plan into a useful 3D visualization

Prepare a readable floor plan, choose a view and verify rooms, doors and proportions in an AI-generated 3D concept before sharing it.

Quick answer

Upload a clear 2D plan to AI Home Planner & Room Design, choose a view and generate a visual concept. Compare the result against the source plan before using it in a presentation. An attractive image is not a measured CAD model.

Step by step

  1. Prepare the complete plan

    Use a scan, screenshot or photo with legible walls, doors and room labels. Straighten a tilted photo and keep the full outline visible. Retain dimensions in the source when available; do not crop away the scale or a room entrance.

  2. Choose a view that answers your question

    A top-down view makes room relationships easier to compare. An angled or isometric view can communicate volume and furnishing ideas. Start with a neutral style so decoration does not distract you from checking the layout.

  3. Check the generated layout against the source

    Count rooms, locate doors and windows, and compare the relative sizes of adjacent spaces. Review circulation routes and stair positions. Reject a variation that changes a structural element or hides an inconsistency, even if it looks polished.

  4. Export a clearly labeled concept

    Compare variations, keep the one that best reflects the plan, and export an image for discussion. Share the original plan alongside it and label the render as an AI-generated visualization.

Example: a two-bedroom apartment

Before choosing finishes, confirm that both bedrooms remain in the correct positions, the bathroom connects to the intended hallway and the kitchen has not absorbed part of another room. Only then compare furniture and color options.

Before you use the result

AI can misinterpret a source or change details. Verify dimensions independently; an exported image is not a construction drawing, editable BIM file or engineering approval. Check the current App Store listing for subscription requirements.
