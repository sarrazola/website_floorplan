Use AI to explore a garden layout before choosing plants

Create a garden concept and check paths, sunlight, space and maintenance against the real site.

Quick answer

AI garden imagery can suggest a visual arrangement, but plant selection and placement need real site information. Use the concept as a starting point for discussion and verify conditions before planting.

Step by step

  1. Record the site conditions

    Keep a photo or plan showing boundaries, paths and existing features. Note sunlight, shade, drainage and how people move through the space.

  2. Choose the main purpose

    Decide whether you need seating, a clear walkway or planting beds. Start with a simple layout so practical conflicts remain visible.

  3. Check the generated arrangement

    Compare path widths and access points with the source. Look for plants or objects that cover drains, doors or required circulation.

  4. Choose real materials and plants separately

    Check mature plant size, climate suitability, maintenance and availability with reliable local guidance. Measure before purchasing.

A practical example

For a narrow patio, compare a seating area with the route to the door. Keep the route clear and check mature plant dimensions before choosing containers.

Before you use the result

Generated plants may be unnamed or unsuitable for the climate. The image does not verify irrigation, drainage, structural loads or plant safety.
